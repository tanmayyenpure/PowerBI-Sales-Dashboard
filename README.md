# 📖 Overview

**PowerBI Sales Analytics** is an interactive **Business Intelligence and Data Visualization project** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** and **Microsoft Excel**.

The project transforms raw transactional sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides a centralized view of important business performance indicators.

The dashboard enables users to analyze sales performance across different:

- Product Categories
- Product Sub-Categories
- Shipping Modes
- States
- Regions
- Customers
- Years
- Discounts
- Delivery Performance

By combining data transformation, KPI analysis, business intelligence, and interactive visualizations, this project demonstrates how raw business data can be converted into meaningful insights for **data-driven decision making**.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Organizations generate large amounts of sales data every day. However, raw datasets alone do not provide an efficient way to understand overall business performance.

Traditional manual analysis can be:

- Time-consuming
- Difficult to interpret
- Repetitive
- Difficult to scale
- Challenging for decision-makers
- Inefficient for identifying trends
- Difficult to visualize

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales records into a centralized and interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard**.

The dashboard allows users to quickly identify important sales trends, product performance, geographical patterns, shipping behavior, and business KPIs.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

The analysis provides several important business insights from the dataset.

---

## 🚀 Technology Leads Sales

Technology is the highest-performing category in the dashboard, generating approximately:

**$836K in sales**

This makes Technology the largest contributor among the three major categories.

---

## 🪑 Furniture Performance

Furniture contributes approximately:

**$742K in sales**

making it the second-largest category by sales.

---

## 📎 Office Supplies

Office Supplies contributes approximately:

**$719K in sales**

and represents another significant portion of the company's overall sales.

---

## 🚚 Standard Class Dominates Shipping

Standard Class is the highest-performing shipping mode, generating approximately:

**$1.36M in sales**

This indicates that customers predominantly use standard shipping for their purchases.

---

## 📱 Phones Are a Top-Performing Sub-Category

Phones generate approximately:

**$330K in sales**

making them one of the strongest-performing product sub-categories.

---

## 📈 Strong Sales Growth

The year-wise sales analysis shows strong growth toward the later years of the dataset.

Sales reached approximately:

**$733K in 2017**

indicating significant improvement compared with earlier years.

---

## 👥 Customer Base

The dataset contains approximately:

**793 unique customers**

This provides a useful foundation for further customer segmentation and customer behavior analysis.

---

## ⏱️ Delivery Performance

The average delivery duration is approximately:

**4 days**

This provides an operational KPI that can be used to evaluate shipping and delivery performance.
